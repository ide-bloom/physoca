# Regulatory Note

## Intended Use

Physoca displays and analyzes a patient's medication information against published prescribing criteria and presents the results, with their sources, to a health care professional who independently reviews the basis for each result. It does not replace clinical judgement and is not intended to diagnose, to select a treatment, or to direct a change in therapy.

## Health Canada SaMD Guidance

Under Health Canada's *Software as a Medical Device: Definition and Classification* guidance (2019; examples revised 2022) and the *Food and Drugs Act* / *Medical Devices Regulations*, software that only supports a health care professional's decision—displaying and analyzing information against published criteria without replacing clinical judgement or directing treatment—is treated differently from software that diagnoses or directs therapy.

Because Physoca is strictly deterministic and rule-based, Health Canada's *Pre-market Guidance for Machine-Learning-Enabled Medical Devices* (February 2025) does not apply.
