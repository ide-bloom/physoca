# Clinical Sources

Physoca's source registry (`src/physoca/rules/sources.yaml`) orders Canadian guidelines, criteria, and toolkits first, followed by international corroborating sources.

Key Canadian sources include:
- McDonald et al. (2026), Canadian primary care guideline on potentially inappropriate prescribing (*Can Fam Physician* 2026;72(3):173-178).
- Deprescribing.org guidelines (PPI, BZRA, antipsychotics, antihyperglycemics, cholinesterase inhibitors/memantine).
- Choosing Wisely Canada recommendations and toolkits.
- Diabetes Canada Chapter 37 (2018) and CCSMH guidelines.

International corroboration includes AGS Beers Criteria (2023), AGS Alternatives (2025), STOPP/START v3 (2023) with Boland et al. (2025) evidence appraisal, and multi-scale anticholinergic burden tables (ACB, CALS, Danish 2024).
